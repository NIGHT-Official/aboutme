SnapShelf:
one folder in,
every platform out.

Sell the same product in more than one place? Drop in your photos, tick the platforms, and get a ready-to-upload set for each one. Your originals are never changed.

Works offline No account No subscription Free version

How it works

Three steps

One run, one folder per platform.

Step 1

Choose photos

Pick a folder or drop files on the window. JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP and TIFF are read.

Step 2

Tick platforms

Etsy, eBay, Shopify, Instagram and Gumroad are built in. The preview shows each platform's size, crop or padding.

Step 3

Export

Each platform gets its own folder of resized, renamed files, with a report of what was done to every photo.

What it takes care of

The fiddly parts

The things that are easy to get wrong by hand.

Rules

Sizes with sources

Every built-in preset records the platform help page it was taken from and the date it was checked. You can copy a preset and change it.

Safety

Originals untouched

Exports always go to a new, dated folder. Nothing is overwritten and your source photos are only ever read.

Names

Tidy file names

Set a naming pattern once and see every new name in a table before you export.

Marks

Watermarks, where allowed

Add a text or logo watermark. It is left off for eBay, whose picture policy does not allow watermarks.

Warnings

Too-small photos flagged

A photo smaller than a platform's size is never stretched. It is exported as it is and pointed out.

Pricing

Free version

Up to 10 photos per run

Every platform and every feature. SnapShelf never adds a watermark of its own to your photos.

Licensed

No limit

One purchase, one license key, for use on two of your computers. The price will be shown here when SnapShelf goes on sale.

Your photos stay on your computer.

SnapShelf does all its work on your own machine. There is no account, no upload and no usage tracking. The only time it goes online is to confirm a license key, and it keeps working when you are offline.
